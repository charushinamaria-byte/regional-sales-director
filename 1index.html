04 · What to expect

Your interview journey

Four video calls, one onsite day in Boston, typically 2–4 weeks end to end. Select a stage to see exactly what happens.

Stage 1 of 6, recruiter screen

HR interview

A relaxed 30–45 minute call with Lily Kovalova, your Senior Talent Partner. No trick questions — we want to understand your leadership background, what motivates you, and make sure the role fits on both sides.

  • Your career journey and what’s driving your move now
  • The teams you’ve led — size, segment, quota and results
  • Compensation expectations, remote setup and timeline
  • Time for your questions about the team, culture or role

💡 Tip: Have a crisp summary of the teams you’ve built — headcount, team quota, attainment — and why you’re exploring a move now. Specifics land well.

Duration
30–45 minutes
Format
Video, MS Teams or Zoom
Who you’ll meet
Lily Kovalova, Senior Talent Partner

Within 3–5 days of applying

Stage 2 of 6, hiring manager

HM intro call

Meet your potential Hiring Manager for a 45-minute to 1-hour conversation about role fit, the region and team you’d lead, and what success looks like in your first six months.

  • Your leadership track record — team attainment, hiring, ramp and retention
  • How you build pipeline and run a forecast across a team
  • Which opening fits you best — Enterprise, Emerging Enterprise or BFSI — and the team you’d lead
  • Your questions — strategy, team, market or the role itself

💡 Tip: Know your team’s numbers cold: attainment by year, % of reps at quota, forecast accuracy, regretted attrition. Hiring managers love commercial clarity.

Stage 3 of 6, sales leadership

CSO call

A 45-minute to 1-hour conversation with our Global Chief Sales Officer. Andy wants to hear your career story in your own words — how you got here, how you lead, and why Creatio, why now.

  • Your career, chronologically — the key moves and the “why” behind each
  • Formative moments that shaped how you sell, lead and build teams
  • How you’d approach the General Business or Financial Services team
  • Open floor: Andy’s own path, the sales organisation, or where Creatio is headed

💡 Tip: Think of this as telling your career story, not pitching a strategy. The “why” behind each transition is what Andy is listening for.

Stage 5 of 6, onsite

Leadership case in Boston

A full day at our Boston headquarters. You’ll present your leadership case to the executive team and meet our C-level managers in person. You’ll receive the full brief in advance, and your recruiter will coordinate your travel.

Market analysis and target strategy

A high-level view of the industry and where Creatio should focus, backed by publicly available data.

Task 2

Sales strategy proposal

How you’d win in the market and build the team to do it — positioning, territories, hiring and enablement.

Task 3

Best-practice sales process

How deals get done from first lead to close, and the operating rhythm you’d use to inspect and uplevel the team.

Throughout the day

Meet the executive team

Conversations with Creatio’s C-level managers, Q&A, and time to get a feel for the office and the people.

💡 Tip: Ground your analysis in public data and show your thinking, not just your conclusions — how you’d win the market and lead the team that does it. Treat the day as two-way: you’re meeting the people you’d build with.

09 · Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is the role remote?

Yes — the role is based in the United States and we’re a remote-first team. Expect regular travel for customer meetings, partner events and time with your team. If you’re near Boston, we’d love to see you at our Seaport District office. 🌊

What are the three openings?

We’re hiring three Sales Directors for our North American team of 30+ AEs. In General Business, a Sales Director for Enterprise and one for Emerging Enterprise, both reporting to Amanda Georgoff. In Financial Services, a Regional Sales Director for BFSI (banking, financial services and insurance), reporting to John Gonzalez, VP of Financial Services Sales.

How big is the team I’d lead?

The ideal team is 6–8 AEs. It’s a mix of leading inherited team members and building from the ground up — so you’ll hire a meaningful part of your team yourself.

How much of the role is hands-on selling?

This is a player-coach role. You’ll lead the team and its forecast, but you’ll also actively participate in key deals, partner engagements and C-suite relationships.

What does the OTE look like?

OTE averages $430K, with a 50/50 base/variable split, uncapped commission and stock options. We’re flexible — the final package depends on seniority, experience and how the conversation goes.

What does the leadership case involve?

A full day at our Boston headquarters. You’ll present a case in three parts — market analysis and target strategy, a sales strategy proposal and a best-practice sales process — and meet our executive team and C-level managers. You receive the brief in advance, and your recruiter coordinates travel.

Are there any assessments?

Yes. Before your call with our CEO, you’ll complete a DISC assessment and the UCAT test. Your recruiter coordinates both and will share instructions and timing.

How long does the process take?

Typically 2–4 weeks from first call to offer — fast enough to respect your time, thorough enough to make a great mutual decision.

How does the sales team perform?

Creatio is ranked #1 on RepVue for comp rating and quota attainment, and 70%+ of reps hit quota. Enterprise deals typically range from $150K–$500K ACV, with sales cycles of 3–12 months.

Who is my point of contact?

Lily Kovalova, our Senior Talent Partner. Lily will answer your questions and guide you through each step, from the first call to the offer.

Ready to lead?

Three openings, one great team. We’d love to hear from you.
